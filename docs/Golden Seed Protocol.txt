# Golden Seed Protocol

## Purpose

The Golden Seed Protocol establishes permanent deterministic benchmark worlds used to validate every major engine change.

The goal is simple:

> Every significant engine improvement must make the simulation better without sacrificing determinism, replay safety, stability, or long-term world health.

The Simulation Harness is the authoritative validator for these benchmarks.

---

# Core Principle

A feature is **not complete** until it passes its Golden Seed benchmark.

Passing unit tests is necessary.

Passing the Simulation Harness is required.

---

# Golden Seed 42 — "The Answer"

> "The Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything."

The primary benchmark world.

Seed:

```text
42
```

Purpose:

A balanced deterministic world representing normal gameplay.

Every major engine feature must successfully complete this benchmark.

Acceptance:

- Deterministic replay
- No critical anomalies
- Stable replay fingerprint
- State budget respected
- Prompt-safe projections preserved
- Authority boundaries preserved
- Emergent behaviour equal to or better than the previous baseline

---

# Harness Authority

The Simulation Harness is considered the engine's health oracle.

If the harness reports a defect:

- Assume the engine is incorrect.
- Do not weaken the harness.
- Do not suppress anomaly detection.
- Do not increase thresholds simply to make reports pass.

The only acceptable harness changes are those correcting demonstrably false-positive detections.

# Philosophy

The Dice Reactions engine is not judged by isolated unit tests.

It is judged by whether an entire simulated world can evolve for hundreds or thousands of deterministic turns without losing coherence.

Every subsystem ultimately answers one question:

> Does Golden Seed 42 still pass?

If the answer is **yes**, the engine has become stronger.

If the answer is **no**, the engine has regressed, regardless of how impressive the new feature appears.
